# Battery Charge Manager 0.4.2 — approved scope

The user approved implementation of the preceding analysis, with an absolute rule:
retain every received observation and every operation until explicit user-authorized
deletion. No automatic thinning, burst suppression, age limit or history-count limit.
A display cache is not the archive. Existing lost measurements cannot be reconstructed.

## Storage
Use a per-entry transactional SQLite raw archive in Home Assistant's .storage folder.
Original imported documents, observations, trace ordering and evaluation revisions are
immutable. Preserve all original fields, even fields not understood by the current
models. An idempotent migration retains the old Store and imports its complete parsed
snapshot before the primary archive is used. Current-session checkpoints are derived
recovery state; updating them must never overwrite observations. Historical traces are
hydrated only for details/reanalysis/export. Active-run samples may stay in RAM, but
are committed incrementally rather than rewriting the historical raw data on each tick.
No deletion API is exposed in this release. Storage failure prevents a new run and
initiates checked switch-off during a run; failure must be visible, not silently purged.

## Operations
End, retain and permit calibration use are independent. Manual completion, Stop,
timeout and safety failure produce exactly one visible attempt with reason, retained
trace and usability. A zero meter or incomplete integration must not prevent checked
shutdown and archival. Repeated completion is idempotent. Legacy aborted calibrations
are recovered as excluded attempts without changing their original archived payloads.

## Measurement and analysis
Observe both changed and unchanged HA state reports. Capture values, units, receipt
and HA report timestamps before queueing work. A heartbeat is a cached observation,
not evidence of a new hardware report. Never claim HA reporting proves a fresh physical
measurement. Preserve invalid observations and gaps. Use covered duration, not full
elapsed duration, for the idle correction of partial integrals. Original quality evidence
must not be upgraded merely by restarting, changing mode or running a later analysis.

Main/taper detection requires observed sustained reference; otherwise show undetermined.
After demonstrated main charging, assess a 20-minute fresh low-load regime using time
weighted mean, time occupancy and bounded pulse energy. Short low-power pulses are not
sustained charging. Missing evidence or renewed main load prevents confirmation. Keep
strict end detection for setups without such a reference. An endpoint denotes an
observed input-power transition, not a measured cell state of charge. Nominal battery
energy never sets, rescales or proves an endpoint or input energy.

Expose total-operation, charge and post-end energy with consistent endpoints and both
sources. Manual retrospective endpoint selection requires a reason and appends an
analysis; it does not make estimates eligible. Missing endpoints and baselines stay
unknown. Idle power for new evidence uses time-weighted mean, not a zero median of
pulses. Legacy baselines keep their original method/limitations. Nominal/input ratios
are labelled comparisons, never efficiencies or capacity measurements.

## UI and release
Both energy paths remain visible in gross/net tiles. All attempts are discoverable,
with details and export. Charts preserve extrema and break at original gaps; chart
reduction never removes raw observations. Explain estimate/partial/accepted and source
selection. Release 0.4.2 directly after local and CI validation; no physical-device test
is claimed. The user's raw export must not be published in the public repository.
